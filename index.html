A1 The shelf

Your whole shelf, sleeve by sleeve.

Artwork, artists, labels, genres and full tracklists by side. Sort by recently played, A–Z or most played, filter by genre, and search by title or artist.

Four ways to fill it.

  1. By hand Works offline

    Title, artist, year, label, genres and artwork from your photos. No connection or account needed.

  2. Search Discogs Discogs

    Pick the matching release and the details, genres, artwork and tracklist fill in. Nothing is saved until you add it.

  3. Scan the barcode Discogs

    Point the camera at the jacket’s barcode to find matching Discogs releases, then choose your exact pressing. Not every pressing has a barcode on Discogs—search by artist and title instead.

  4. Import your collection Discogs

    Bring in the vinyl from your Discogs collection. Review new records and possible duplicates first, then import them to your phone.

Discogs features are optional and need an internet connection and a connected Discogs account.

Records you add are saved on your phone. Groovefolio doesn’t send your collection back to Discogs.

A2 The spin

Put the needle down. Keep the listen.

Log a full album or a single side, right now or for a listen earlier on. Every play builds your listening history, record by record.

  • Full album, Side A or Side B Log exactly what you played.
  • Any date and time Forgot to log last night’s record? Set when it played.
  • A history for every record Times played and when you last played it, on each album.
  • A guided first listen The in-app walkthrough takes you through saving a real play.

Optional · NFC tags

Or just tap the sleeve.

Link a writable NFC sticker to a record once. After that, tap your phone to the sleeve and Groovefolio logs a full-album play.

  1. Link it once

    From a record’s page, write its link to a blank tag in the app.

  2. Stick it on the sleeve

    On the jacket or outer sleeve—never on the vinyl itself.

  3. Tap when you play

    You’ll get a “Play logged” confirmation. If the app is open, Undo is right there for accidental taps.

No tags needed—you can always log by hand. Tap-to-log needs an NFC-enabled Android phone, unlocked, with NFC turned on and Groovefolio installed, plus a writable NDEF tag (NTAG215 stickers are tested). Each tap logs the full album; the tag can’t tell what’s actually playing. Use compatible tags you already own—no purchase is required.

B2 Rediscover

Fall for your own shelf again.

Discover suggests records you already own: matches for the genres you’ve been playing lately, your favourite artists and eras, and the ones that haven’t had a spin in a while.

  • Every pick explained Open “Why this record?” to see exactly which signals matched.
  • From your shelf, not a store Recommendations come from records you own. Nothing is being sold to you.
  • Calculated on your phone Built from your collection and logged plays. No recommendation server is involved.

B3 Yours

Your records live on your phone.

  • No Groovefolio account. Open the app and start adding records. There’s nothing to sign up for.
  • Stored on this device. Your collection, artwork and listening history are saved on your phone and aren’t uploaded to a Groovefolio server. Browse your shelf, log plays, and use Stats and Discover offline.
  • No ads. No tracking. No advertising, and no developer-run analytics or crash reporting. (The optional barcode scanner uses Google ML Kit, which sends Google technical diagnostics.)
  • Discogs only when you choose. Connecting Discogs is optional. When you do, searches, barcode lookups and imports go through the Groovefolio server to Discogs—your local collection and plays stay on your phone.

Worth knowing

Local also means looking after it. There’s no cloud backup or sync between devices, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes your records and listening history.

Before the first spin

Can I download Groovefolio yet?

Not yet. It’s being prepared for its first public Android release, and the download link will be added to this page when it’s available.

Do I need a Discogs account?

No. You can add and manage records by hand. Connecting Discogs is optional and turns on search, autofill, barcode lookup and collection import, which all need an internet connection.

Do I need NFC tags?

No. NFC is an optional shortcut for logging a play, and you can log every listen by hand. For tap-to-log you’ll need an NFC-enabled Android phone and a writable NDEF-compatible tag.

What about iPhone?

The first release is Android only. There’s no iPhone release date to announce.
