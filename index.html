ON DEVICE

Local-first by design

Your collection should still exist when the internet doesn't.

Groovefolio's core collection and listening data live locally on your device. A Groovefolio account is not required for the core app, and online integrations such as Discogs are optional enhancements rather than the foundation.

Roadmap

Where the grooves lead next.

The current development build already includes collection management, play logging, stats, tracklists, barcode lookup, Discogs import, onboarding, quick actions, and explainable Discover recommendations. The next work moves toward physical interaction, deeper listening stories, and release readiness.

  1. CURRENT
    Release data

    Discogs + barcode + tracklists

    Search exact vinyl releases, scan a barcode, autofill editable metadata, import a connected collection, and keep side-grouped tracklists locally.

  2. CURRENT
    Collection experience

    Onboarding + quick actions

    First-run guidance teaches the app, while swipe actions make everyday Edit and Delete workflows faster without cluttering the collection.

  3. CURRENT
    Discovery

    Explainable recommendations

    Build a taste profile from local plays and surface rediscovery, genre, and favorite-era picks with a reason for each recommendation.

  4. NEXT
    Physical interaction

    NFC play logging

    Use the existing NFC persistence groundwork to connect a tagged physical record with a fast on-device play-log flow.

  5. FUTURE
    Listening stories

    Album Wrapped + deeper shelf analytics

    Turn long-term play history into yearly stories, shelf coverage, dormant-record insights, rotation depth, and other collection-versus-listening views.
