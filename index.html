The difference

Discogs tells you what you own.
Groovefolio tells you how you listen.

Ownership is the starting point. Groovefolio keeps plays beside the collection, builds a local taste profile, and turns those signals into stats and explainable recommendations from records you already own.

Local-first by design

Your collection works
without a Groovefolio account.

The core shelf, artwork paths, play history, stats, and recommendation inputs live on the device. Discogs is an optional metadata connection—not the place Groovefolio stores your listening life.
