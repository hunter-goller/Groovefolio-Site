LOCAL

Local-first by design

Your collection should still exist when the internet doesn't.

Groovefolio's core collection and listening data live locally on your device. A Groovefolio account is not required for the core app, and online integrations such as Discogs are optional enhancements rather than the foundation.

Roadmap

Where the grooves lead next.

Groovefolio already has collection management, play logging, listening stats, Discogs search/autofill, and collection import in development builds. The next passes deepen metadata, physical interaction, and discovery.

  1. AVAILABLE
    Discogs foundation

    Search + metadata autofill

    Connect Discogs, search vinyl releases, select a pressing, and populate Add Record.

  2. AVAILABLE
    Collection onboarding

    Import a Discogs collection

    Review an existing Discogs collection, avoid exact duplicates, and store selected vinyl releases locally.

  3. NEXT
    Richer metadata

    Tracklists + barcode lookup

    Add track-level data and make identifying physical releases faster.

  4. PLANNED
    Physical interaction

    NFC play logging

    Tap a tagged record to move from the shelf to a logged play with minimal friction.

  5. FUTURE
    Discovery

    Explainable recommendations

    Use collection, genre, artist, track, and play-history signals to suggest what to hear next—and explain why.
