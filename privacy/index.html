Groovefolio is developed by Hunter Goller. This policy explains how the Groovefolio Android app and the public website at groovefolio.app handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact support.groovefolio@gmail.com.

1. At a glance

Groovefolio helps you organize vinyl records, log listens, view listening statistics, and discover records in your own collection. You do not need a Groovefolio account.

Your collection and listening history are stored on your device. Groovefolio does not upload them to a Groovefolio server or provide cloud synchronization or automatic collection backup. Optional online features communicate with Discogs. Barcode scanning uses Google ML Kit, which processes camera images on your device and sends technical metrics to Google as described below.

Groovefolio does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app does not include advertising SDKs or a separate developer-operated behavioral analytics or crash-reporting service.

2. Information stored and used on your device

Groovefolio stores information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Collection information: record titles, artists, release details, genres, tracks, artwork, Discogs release references, and any optional record information you enter.
  • Listening history: records played, play dates and times, and full-album or side selections. Stats and Discover use this information locally to calculate summaries and recommendations.
  • Preferences: appearance settings and walkthrough progress.
  • NFC information: tag identifiers and their associations with records, together with information about when tags were linked.
  • Discogs authorization information: tokens used to maintain your optional Discogs connection.

Selected artwork is copied into app storage. Groovefolio does not upload your selected photos or scan your photo library. Downloaded artwork is also stored locally.

The developer cannot remotely view, restore, or delete information held only on your device.

3. Optional Discogs features

If you connect Discogs, you authorize Groovefolio through Discogs in your browser. Groovefolio receives authorization tokens and account identity information, such as your Discogs username and account identifier. Groovefolio does not receive your Discogs password. Tokens are stored using the device's secure credential storage.

The app communicates directly with Discogs over HTTPS for account verification, release searches, barcode lookups, release details, collection imports, and artwork downloads. Depending on the feature you use, Discogs receives authorization information, your search terms, barcode values, release identifiers, or your username for collection requests. Discogs and its image-serving infrastructure also receive normal connection information, including your IP address and request metadata.

When you import a collection, Groovefolio retrieves collection and release information from Discogs and saves your selected records locally. Your local play history and locally made collection changes are not synchronized back to Discogs.

Discogs operates independently and handles information under its Privacy Policy. Its authorization website may use cookies and other browser technologies under that policy.

You can use manual record entry and local features without connecting Discogs. Disconnecting in Groovefolio removes locally stored Discogs authorization credentials; it does not delete your Discogs account, delete imported local records, or revoke authorization on Discogs' systems. Manage or revoke that authorization separately through Discogs.

4. Barcode scanning and Google ML Kit

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a record's barcode. Barcode recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit through the app's scanner component. Camera frames are not saved by Groovefolio or uploaded for recognition. The decoded barcode value is sent to Discogs when the app searches for matching releases.

Google ML Kit sends technical data to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. This includes device and operating-system details, app/package versions, an SDK installation identifier, performance measurements, scanner configuration, input/output sizes, feature versions, event types, and error codes. With automatic zoom enabled, it also collects a generated scan-session identifier, zoom changes, and estimated barcode bounding-box coordinates.

Google states that ML Kit processes input images and recognition results on-device, while technical metrics are transmitted over HTTPS. The SDK may also contact Google for maintenance information and updates. See ML Kit's privacy information, its Android data disclosures, and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can decline or revoke camera permission and enter records manually instead.

5. NFC tags and notifications

If you choose to link an NFC tag, Groovefolio reads the tag identifier, stores its association with a record locally, and writes a Groovefolio link containing that record's local identifier to the physical tag. The link does not contain your Discogs credentials or listening history. NFC scans and play logging are handled locally.

A physical NFC tag can be read by someone with a compatible reader. Deleting app data or a record does not erase the physical tag; erase or rewrite the tag separately if you want to remove its contents.

If you allow notifications, Groovefolio can display locally generated confirmations of NFC plays. These can contain a record title, artwork, and play details. Their visibility depends on your Android notification and lock-screen settings. These confirmations are not delivered through a Groovefolio push-notification server.

You can manage camera and notification permissions in Android Settings and disable NFC through your device settings. Core manual collection and play-logging features remain available without these permissions.

6. Support messages

When you email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your name, message, screenshots, device model, or app version. This information is used to respond, investigate problems, maintain the app, and address privacy requests. Support messages are handled through Google's Gmail service under the Google Privacy Policy.

Please do not send passwords, authorization tokens, or information unrelated to your request. Groovefolio does not automatically attach your collection or listening history to support emails.

Support correspondence is retained while handling your request and afterwards only for as long as reasonably needed for related follow-up, resolving disputes, or meeting applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the support address. If information must be retained for a legal reason, the developer will explain the applicable limitation.

7. Website and external links

The public Groovefolio website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website does not provide access to your app collection or listening history. The website does not include advertising trackers or third-party visitor analytics.

If you follow an external link from the app or website, the destination receives normal web-request information and operates under its own privacy policy. This includes links to Discogs, retailers, and help resources. Simply opening such a link does not upload your local collection or listening history.

8. Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app's storage. You can delete a record within Groovefolio; this also removes its associated play history and local NFC association. The app attempts to remove its stored artwork copy as part of record deletion. Clearing all app storage removes remaining app-managed files and preferences.

To remove all locally held Groovefolio data, use Android Settings to clear Groovefolio's storage or uninstall the app. This does not delete original images in your photo library, information held by Discogs or Google, support correspondence, or data written on physical NFC tags.

Groovefolio disables Android automatic backup for its app-private data and does not provide a Groovefolio cloud backup. Uninstalling, clearing storage, losing your phone, or moving to another device can permanently lose your collection and listening history.

Discogs, Google, and GitHub determine retention and deletion for data they handle under their respective policies. Contact them for requests concerning that data. There is no Groovefolio account to delete.

9. Security and your choices

Groovefolio uses app-private storage for local records, secure credential storage for Discogs tokens, and HTTPS for Discogs API and artwork requests. Local collection storage is not a separately password-protected or app-encrypted database. Protect your device with its security and screen-lock features. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

You can choose whether to connect Discogs, use the camera, select artwork, link NFC tags, or enable notifications. To request deletion of support emails and attachments held by the developer, contact support.groovefolio@gmail.com. Collection and listening data are stored on your device and must be deleted through the app or Android Settings. Questions about support information, or requests to access or correct it, can also be sent to this address. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. The developer will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify that the request concerns your information.

Third-party providers may process information in countries outside your own. Their privacy policies explain their processing locations and applicable protections.

10. Children

Groovefolio is a general-audience vinyl collection app and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact support.groovefolio@gmail.com so the developer can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

11. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Groovefolio’s data practices change, with the revised effective date identified. Material changes will be communicated through the app or website as appropriate, with any additional notice or consent required by law. New server-based features would require an updated disclosure before they are introduced.
